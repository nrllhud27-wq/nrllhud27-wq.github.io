## Project Overview

This project analyzes 990,236 validated transactions (2023–2024) from a chocolate manufacturer operating 200 SKUs across 100 stores in 6 countries. Management suspected that high-cocoa products were mispriced and that certain stores were structurally underperforming.

The data did not support either hypothesis. Gross margin stayed consistently around 40% across product categories, cocoa tiers, and countries, and discount depth showed no meaningful correlation with sales volume in underperforming stores (Pearson r = 0.0047).
